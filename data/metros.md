# Metro areas with several airports

When a question names a city or metro area instead of a specific airport, `search_airports` returns every
airport listed here so the agent can show the list and ask which one to use. One `##` section per metro, the
heading holds the name and its common short forms in parentheses, bullets are `CODE - airport name` with the
primary commercial airport first. Codes must exist in the T-100 extract.

## Los Angeles (LA)
- LAX - Los Angeles International
- BUR - Hollywood Burbank
- LGB - Long Beach
- ONT - Ontario International
- SNA - John Wayne (Orange County)

## Chicago
- ORD - O'Hare International
- MDW - Midway International

## New York (NYC)
- JFK - John F. Kennedy International
- LGA - LaGuardia
- EWR - Newark Liberty International

## Washington (DC)
- DCA - Reagan National
- IAD - Dulles International
- BWI - Baltimore/Washington International

## San Francisco Bay Area (SF)
- SFO - San Francisco International
- OAK - Oakland International
- SJC - San Jose International

## Houston
- IAH - George Bush Intercontinental
- HOU - William P. Hobby

## Dallas
- DFW - Dallas/Fort Worth International
- DAL - Dallas Love Field

## South Florida (Miami)
- MIA - Miami International
- FLL - Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International
- PBI - Palm Beach International
