# Curated airport notes

Hand-written facts the public data cannot show, each with a source. One `##` section per IATA code, one `###`
block per note whose heading is the note type: `constraint` (counts as an unmet-demand flag) or `context`.
The paragraph is the note text; `source:` and `verified:` lines are metadata. Answers label these as "curated note".

## SNA

### constraint
One commercial runway (20R/2L, about 5,700 ft) and the John Wayne Airport Settlement Agreement: annual passengers capped at 11.8 million through 2025 and 12.2 million for 2026-2030 (conditional), daily commercial departures limited, and no commercial departures 10 pm-7 am through 2035. Capacity here is legal before it is physical.
source: https://newportbeachca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/58737/636565322849330000
verified: 2026-10-18

## SFO

### constraint
Two pairs of parallel runways only 750 ft apart (independent parallel approaches need 4,300 ft). In 2025 the FAA banned side-by-side visual approaches, cutting the good-weather arrival rate from about 54 to about 36 per hour; low visibility already forced single-stream arrivals. Four runways behave like two for arrivals.
source: https://ops.group/blog/san-francisco-side-by-side-parallel-visual-approaches-banned/
verified: 2026-10-18

## DCA

### constraint
Slot-controlled under the FAA High Density Rule (60 IFR operations per hour, 6 am-midnight) and a 1,250-statute-mile perimeter rule (49 U.S.C. 49109) with a small number of congressionally granted beyond-perimeter exemptions. Added capacity cannot be used without an act of Congress.
source: https://www.flyreagan.com/dca/dca-reagan-national-slot-perimeter-rules
verified: 2026-10-18

## ANC

### context
World's #3 cargo airport in ACI's 2025 ranking (#4 in 2024) because roughly 80% of trans-Pacific freighters make a technical stop here to refuel and change crews. Most all-cargo departures are long-haul transit flights, not local demand; read the cargo long-haul share with that in mind.
source: https://www.alaskasnewssource.com/2026/04/19/ted-stevens-international-airport-now-ranks-1st-us-cargo-nears-top-worldwide-list/
verified: 2026-10-18
